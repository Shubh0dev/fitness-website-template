FAQ

Before your first session

Anything else? Call or WhatsApp +91 95061 54321.

What happens in the free trial?

Your free trial session includes a thorough intake, an assessment and goal setting. You also get a free diet chart on your visit.

I'm a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Yes. Yoga has Hatha Yoga for Beginners, aerial yoga classes cater to all levels, and Pilates is low-impact and suitable for all ages and fitness levels. The assessment in your trial makes sure training starts at your level.

How much does membership cost?

Prices aren't published online. Ask on WhatsApp or call, and the team will share current fees for your centre and programme.

What are the class timings?

They vary by centre and programme. Yoga, for example, has morning and evening classes. Ask the team for the current timetable at your centre.

Is Karma for women and men?

Yes. Karma by Satwik is a unisex studio.

Do I need to bring a yoga mat?

No. Mats are available at the studio, though you're welcome to bring your own.

I have an injury or a health condition. Can I join?

Please check with your doctor first. Then tell the trainers at your intake: Karma's assessment covers your goals, medical history and lifestyle.

What happens after I send the form?

The form opens WhatsApp with your details filled in. Once you press send, the team replies there to arrange your trial.
